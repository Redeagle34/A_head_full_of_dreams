# SHIFT demonstrates a reusable regional and seasonal conversion of MODIS activity to a VIIRS reference scale.

The Apache-2.0 repository bins records into active cell-days and scales historic counts from overlap data. Extrapolation before VIIRS is unverified. Compare against a shared-grid baseline and report out-of-sample errors by season and region.
