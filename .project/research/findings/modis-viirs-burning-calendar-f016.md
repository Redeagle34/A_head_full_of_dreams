# LANDFIRE vegetation and fuels maps support landscape-scale assessment in the U.S.

LANDFIRE publishes data on a 30 m grid but advises working at a larger spatial scale. Aggregate fuel and vegetation classes to analysis areas and select a release available before any retrospective forecast issuance.
