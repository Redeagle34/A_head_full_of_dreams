# NASA HLS provides common-grid 30 m reflectance for vegetation monitoring

The product combines Landsat and Sentinel-2, with nominal acquisitions every 2–3 days. Cloud-screen usable scenes and keep the age of the most recent image. Test whether time-varying vegetation measures improve the seven-day detection forecast.
