# Landsat 8 and 9 documented the Park Fire in Northern California in July 2024

USGS publishes imagery from July 26 and 27. The pair provides a documented incident for the imagery workflow. Per-scene access, the corresponding archive records and a cloud-screened pre-fire scene remain to be checked.
