# ECMWF evaluated weather-and-fuel forecasts against MODIS active fire detections.

The paper demonstrates a daily forecast and historical comparisons with seasonal climatology. A regional seven-day probability target needs its own evaluation. Use forecast weather available at issue time rather than future realized weather.
