# A historical record spanning instruments must account for changes in platforms and processing versions.

FIRMS lists S-NPP through 2026-11-02 and NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 as continuing. A live next-week forecast needs a fixed current observing stream and evaluation against that stream. Standard archive labels and near-real-time inputs can differ.
