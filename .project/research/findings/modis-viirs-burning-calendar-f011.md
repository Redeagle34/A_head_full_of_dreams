# A Bolivia preprint tests seven-day VIIRS detection probabilities per grid cell against seasonal frequency and persistence.

The authors report test average precision of 0.323 for LightGBM and 0.167 for seasonal frequency. The findings come from one country and S-NPP, and their outage rule does not establish uniform viewing conditions. Artifact downloads and local transfer are unverified.
