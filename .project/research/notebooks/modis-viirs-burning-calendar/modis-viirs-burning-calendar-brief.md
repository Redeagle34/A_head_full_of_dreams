## User's approach

We are currently in the ideation stage. We have thought about how the satelites work (which seems to be through Thermal Infrared Sensors) and seeing the heat signatures from generated fires. This helps to detect, but makes it hard to differentiate between which fires were generated by humans (and are thus created to control possible worse fires) and which fires are really not expected and should be controlled. We have also investigated about including data from airplanes (which in aggregation to the data from the satellites could possibly let us make better estimates).
