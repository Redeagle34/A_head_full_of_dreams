    Fires around the red dot

    The red dot on the Earth is the centre of this card, and the dashed ring is how far it looks. Drag the Earth to move them. The card lists the fires the satellites saw inside the ring in the dates of the time bar, and how dangerous each one was. Fold the card and the dot goes away.

    What the danger levels mean

    The level is the chance that a fire grows past 1,000 acres, judged after the VIIRS satellites have watched it for two days. The table shows what happened at each level in 2022 to 2025. It suggests where to look first; it is not a dispatch order.

    The year here

    The ring is one year, January at the top. A petal is a week: its length is how much usually burns then. Red petals are the usual fire season. Blue dots are the chosen year; red dots are the dates on the map. Pick a petal to see that week on the map.
